# NEXORA — Session 07 Report: Transportation, Mobility & Smart Infrastructure Engine

**Status: COMPLETE** — the physical movement layer was built by EXTENDING Sessions 01–06 (never recreating a system): citizens' days, clinic journeys and freight already existed as logical records; this session records the journeys themselves on Session 03's real road graphs and lets the journeys push back through congestion, road condition and closures. Two new modules (`tools/sim/transport.py`, `tools/sim/transportview.py`), one new config file (`data/transport-config.json`), additive edits to five existing files.

## What was built

- **Vehicles as persistent entities** (`transport.py`, `vehicles` table) — every vehicle has a type from `data/vehicles.json`, an owner (citizen / business / SYS-MARKET), a zone, a fuel quantity, an odometer, a condition and a status. Genesis seeds what the world already implies: 5 buses at their real terminal buildings, one truck per producing/distributing business (28 at 242-agent genesis), 2 SYS-MARKET carrier trucks per city, and a rented cab for every employed taxi driver — the cab the monthly settlement already charges them 200 NCr/day for. A monthly purchase sweep lets employed adults buy at `prices.json` prices through the ledger (bicycle → rickshaw → motorbike → car_small → car_premium by balance; taxi drivers excluded — they rent by design). 56 vehicles were bought organically in the 90-day benchmark (1,379,000 NCr of ledger purchases).
- **Trips from actual demand** — each day, consecutive schedule blocks in different zones become a recorded journey: origin zone, destination zone, purpose (from the destination block's activity), departure minute, route (weighted Dijkstra over the real graph on the mode's own road rights from vehicles.json), distance, free-flow time, congested travel time, arrival minute, fare, fuel. Mode choice weighs distance, cost, time and ownership: own vehicle → walk (≤1,200 m) → bus (if a route serves the pair, in operating hours) → rickshaw (≤3,500 m) → taxi (if the city has an employed taxi driver and the citizen can pay) → walk. Session 06's patient visits generate healthcare trips to the real facility zone the same day.
- **Traffic that emerges from movement** — the day's vehicle trips are tallied per (segment, hour) in passenger-car equivalents; the congestion factor (config SIM MODEL: `1 + 3.0 × flow/capacity`, capped 3.0, against roads.py's class capacities) is persisted in `road_loads` as the day's measured traffic. Today's travel times use today's factors; tomorrow's route choices use today's peak factors (drivers plan around the worst hour they know). Nothing is randomised: the same route at a loaded hour is measurably slower (corridor proof in the congestion tests: 40 taxis in one hour on one OD raise the probe traveller's time and put factor > 1.1 loads on record; the organic 242-agent world peaks at factor 1.0814 on `seg_industry_local`).
- **Road state feeds routing** — Session 03's segment condition slows travel (`1 + (100 − condition)/100 × 0.5`), so Session 05's infrastructure-policy repairs measurably speed routes up; a segment under condition 20 or explicitly closed (`road_segments.closed`, new column) is impassable and routes detour (test-proven).
- **Public transit** — two bus routes (Rupsha City Loop, 6 stops; Shibnagar Town Bus, 4 stops) routed as cycles over the graph on the bus class's road rights, with headways, operating hours and hourly capacity (passengers × runs/hour from vehicles.json). Boardings are recorded per trip; hourly runs are recorded in `transit_runs` with boardings vs capacity; riders beyond capacity spill to rickshaw/walk with the spill on record. Intercity commuters ride an intercity bus over the bridge connection. The Session 03 ferry (RC-FERRY-1, both ghats are real buildings) serves ghat-to-ghat journeys at the config's crossing time.
- **Freight made physical** — every Session 04 supply order and every intercity trade flow now also writes a `freight_shipments` row: seller → buyer (or city → city for trade), the seller's truck or a carrier truck, the routed path, departure at the settlement minute, congested arrival, and delay vs free flow. Shipments flip to `delivered` in the daily pass once their arrival minute passes. The money is untouched: Session 04's shipping charge remains the only freight payment (trade shipments carry freight_ncr 0 — the tariff is Session 04 trade's own business).
- **Energy** — motor trips consume fuel by vehicles.json's per-100 km figures, deducted from the vehicle's tank; vehicles below the reserve fraction refuel (a `vehicle_events` record; quantities only — **no fuel price exists in prices.json, so no trip is ever charged for fuel**; owners' running costs remain their monthly upkeep band). Monthly maintenance wears vehicle condition with distance and services it under a threshold (recorded event; no per-event price exists either).
- **Emergency mobility** — a Session 06 off-district referral admission creates an ambulance trip the same day: facility zone → the city's bridge gate on motor roads, travel time at half the ordinary congestion/condition load (priority, SIM MODEL), destination recorded as `off_district` with the admission id on the record. There is no ambulance class in vehicles.json, so the trip references the journey, not an invented vehicle row.
- **Transport economics** — taxi fares are charged per trip at prices.json `taxi_fares` (base + per-km; its own surge conditions — rain presets, night hours — at its own multiplier) from rider to SYS-MARKET, with transfer keys and day event refs (349,256 NCr over the 90-day benchmark; taxi drivers' income stays the settlement wage, so no fare is counted twice). Bus/rickshaw/ferry travel is covered by the monthly `transport_public_bus_rickshaw` living line prices.json already charges — fare 0 at point of use, recorded as such. In government worlds the municipal treasuries pay SYS-MARKET a per-boarding transit subsidy (SIM MODEL 15 NCr, capped 60,000/city/month, only what the treasury holds): 175,695 NCr over the benchmark's 3 months.
- **Environmental outputs** — every motor trip records its fuel; emissions (config SIM MODEL factors per fuel type) ride the trip's details; the monthly `transport_statistics` snapshots (per city + national) aggregate trips by mode, distance, boardings, fares, fuel, emissions, shipments, delays and the month's maximum congestion factor, ready for Session 08.
- **Observatory** — `--transport` CLI + JSON (`transportview.py`): vehicles by type/acquisition, trips by mode, fares, congestion hotspots from `road_loads`, transit routes with boardings/load factors, freight totals, latest monthly statistics, and the methodology notes. Data layer only.

## Limitations (honest)

- **Rail, air and maritime operations do not run.** A Railway Station plot and a Launch Terminal exist as *buildings*, but no rail track network, timetable, airfield or port data exists anywhere in the merged data, and none was fabricated. The spec's rail/air units are therefore config-framework only in the honest sense: there is nothing to extend. The river ferry DOES run — Session 03 persisted the RC-FERRY-1 connection and both ghats are real buildings.
- **Ferry boardings are 0 organically.** The ferry serves ghat-to-ghat journeys, but no profession's workplace and no generated schedule produces that exact OD pair (ghat zones host no workplace; Shibnagar homes are in c2_residential). The machinery is test-proven with a fabricated ghat-to-ghat schedule; in lived worlds the bridge carries intercity travel. This is a data-shape fact, not a hidden gap.
- **Organic congestion is modest** (max factor 1.08) at 242 citizens against arterial-scale capacities. The response curve is proven under corridor load in tests and in the load tally; a denser world (or Session 10's scale work) is where it bites. No capacity figure was bent to manufacture drama.
- **Freight arrival does not re-time Session 04's inventory settlement.** Goods settle in Session 04's pass (its tests pin that behaviour); the shipment record is the journey's audit trail — route, vehicle, congested arrival, delay — created from the same orders. Making shelf availability wait on arrival would rewrite Session 04's contract and is recorded here as the deliberate boundary.
- **No traffic signals, queueing or incident model.** roads.py carries no signal data and Session 06 already owns commute incidents as health causes; inventing either would violate the no-fabrication rule. Signal timing/queues from the spec remain unimplemented for lack of data.
- **Monthly statistics are month-end snapshots**: a month's own shipments read as in-transit in that month's snapshot (they arrive hours after the boundary); live totals in the observatory and summary are always current.
- **Intra-zone movement is not recorded** (trips exist between zones; a clinic visit inside one's own zone leaves no network journey), and off-district workplaces (the pilot gap) generate no trips.
