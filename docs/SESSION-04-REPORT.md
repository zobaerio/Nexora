## The traceability spine (foundation for all 16 units)

`Store.transfer()` now carries, on every row: both endpoints (character **or business** on either side), amount, reason code, an **idempotency key** (`transfer_key`, unique index — replaying a key returns the original row and moves nothing twice) and the **causing event** (`event_ref`: a month tag, a loan id, a decision tag, a trade-flow id). Business endpoints are balance-enforced exactly like characters. The ledger is **append-only, enforced by database triggers** (`trg_ledger_no_update` / `trg_ledger_no_delete`, added in `_migrate()` so existing worlds gain them too) — history cannot be rewritten. `Store.ledger_sum_check()` now reconciles business caches as well as character caches, and `finance.verify_conservation()` checks the whole book: exactly one source and one destination per row, positive amounts, unique keys, zero cache mismatches. A new system account `SYS-BANK` joins `SYS-CITY` / `SYS-EMPLOYERS` / `SYS-MARKET`.

## Units

1. **Economic Foundation** (`finance.py`, `data/economy-config.json`) — the two cities are regions of one national economy; per-city characteristics (population, development, trade balance) are derived from records at call time. Currency is configuration (NCr; the config asserts it matches `prices.json`'s canonical currency). The economy's event history is the business + city event logs merged chronologically — no invented chronicle.
2. **Individual Finance** (`finance.py`) — a per-person profile computed **from the ledger**, never stored: income/expenses by reason over a trailing window, cash, bank deposits, property value, business equity, portfolio value, debt (loans + household arrears), monthly obligations, purchasing power, net worth. Impossible transactions are rejected by construction (non-system senders cannot go below zero; a failed transfer writes nothing — atomicity is tested).
3. **Labour Market** (`labour.py`) — vacancies (business + civic), applications with deterministic scores, hiring/firing/resignation. Employers pay from **real accounts**: genesis employments stay on `SYS-EMPLOYERS`; labour-market hires carry `employer_business_id` and are paid by the business itself at settlement (`_pay_business_wages`, keyed per employment+month), taxed on what was actually received; a shortfall is recorded as a `wage_shortfall` business event. An employer whose balance cannot cover the wage-reserve rule **cannot post a vacancy**. Credential gates from Session 02 are reused unchanged. Unemployment is measured (18–64, not enrolled) per city and nationally.
4. **Businesses** (`business.py`) — real entities in **real commercial buildings**: Session 04 materialises every leasable commercial unit (shops/offices/factories/farmland from the canonical buildings data, civic institutions excluded) as a `properties` row with `status='commercial'`; each business sits in one. Owner-profession firms (shop_owner/business_owner) are founded for their owners with tier seed capital; remaining units are seeded with **system-founded firms, labelled as such** in their registration events, in proportion to each city's current households (SIM MODEL cap 1.5 firms/household, min 6/city, producer categories first) — units beyond the cap stay vacant rather than conjuring solvent shops in a demand vacuum. Full lifecycle: capital (ledger), employees, wage bill, commercial rent (system firms only — owner firms' rent already lives in the settlement P&L; never charged twice), monthly P&L, distress (balance < next obligations) and closure after 2 consecutive distressed months with layoffs + inventory liquidation, all as recorded events.
5. **Production** (`market.py`, `data/production-recipes.json`) — six recipes over six goods (`data/economy-goods.json`): farm produce, fishing, food processing, light manufacturing, garment making, supplies packing. A run consumes real inventory + crew and pays materials from the business account (prices anchored to `prices.json` where the design prices them; unanchored figures are labelled SIM MODEL in the data file). Primary producers idle when their storehouse holds 3 runs' worth (they do not pay materials to pile up unsold output). Storm months halve farm/fishing output, using the sim's existing weather.
6. **Supply Chain** (`market.py`) — stocking businesses order toward a target derived from **recorded** city demand (last month's consumption + unmet + producer input use; a population baseline stands in only before any consumption exists, and is labelled). Orders draw from a pass-start stock pool (goods move downstream exactly once per pass — no circulation, no sideways trades within a category except production inputs, end sellers never supply). Shipping is charged per unit-km over the Session 03 road graphs (intercity adds the bridge leg); an order never spends more than half the buyer's balance on one good.
7. **Markets & Pricing** (`market.py`) — a dynamic layer over `prices.json` base prices: one bounded step per good per city per month driven by the coverage ratio (stocked supply / recorded demand), clamped to a floor/cap band around base. **Every change persists its signal values** (supply, demand, coverage, base) in `price_history`. Shortages and surpluses are measured outcomes, not scripts.
8. **Banking** (`banking.py`) — one system bank, capitalised with a labelled genesis grant (5,000,000 NCr, SIM MODEL) and lending **only what it holds**. Accounts are layered on the ledger: the reconciliation identity *account balance == deposits − withdrawals* is verified by `account_reconciliation()` and holds in every run. Loans use `prices.json`'s **design** terms (bank loan 12%/yr, ≤5× monthly income, 24 months; mortgage 9%/yr, 20% deposit, 20 years; savings interest 4%/yr) — credit assessment reads the applicant's real ledger wage history, debt and missed payments. Amortising repayments with a keep-back reserve; 3 misses default; a defaulted mortgage forecloses (the household stays as the bank's tenant at the canonical rent). Withdrawals are capped by account balance **and** bank liquidity.
9. **Investment** (`banking.py`) — equity in real businesses only (bonds/funds are not modelled: there are no honest issuers for them in the data). Documented valuation: **net worth = cash + inventory at current market prices** (premises are rented, goodwill is not claimed), split over 100 shares. Dividends are paid monthly from the latest **recorded** profit at a labelled 30% payout ratio, pro-rata, capped so the firm keeps its wage reserve. Simulated only; no real money anywhere.
10. **Government Finance** (`govfinance.py`) — the tax system is a configuration view that **references** `prices.json` (income brackets, business 8%, property 0.5%/yr) and adds two labelled SIM MODEL rates: sales tax 5% (a split of household purchase payments — households pay exactly what they paid before; the share routes to `SYS-CITY` as `sales_tax`) and import tariff 5%. Property tax is **not** duplicated: it still flows only through Session 03's services path; `government_finance` is a reporting snapshot (it writes no ledger rows — tested) attributing national taxes to the capital's treasury account and splitting only locatable revenue (rent share, commercial rent, pensions) per city. Public debt is explicit: `max(0, −SYS-CITY balance)`.
11. **Indicators** (`indicators.py`) — monthly snapshots (national + per city) with a published methodology dict: GDP (income approach: wages + pre-tax business profit + production taxes over the ledger window), GDP/capita, unemployment, inflation (from the Unit 07 basket price index), productivity, median household income, consumer spending, business investment, government revenue, trade volume/balance, poverty (income below the priced subsistence basket: lower-band living costs + tier-1 rent), wealth distribution (Gini + p10/p50/p90 of ledger balances). Every figure is computed from records at snapshot time.
12. **Crises** (`crises.py`) — detection from **measured** conditions only: unemployment > 25%, monthly inflation > 8%, ≥5 business closures in a month, bank reserve ratio below the configured floor (all SIM MODEL thresholds in `economy-config.json`; the scenarios list is an empty, documented config door — no scenario is preloaded). Propagation is mechanical: an unemployment/inflation crisis freezes business hiring; a banking stress halts new lending; a demand shock scales household wants and intercity trade movement. A crisis resolves after 2 consecutive clean months. Consequences are the recorded indicator/business series, not narration.
13. **Trade** (`trade.py`) — Session 03's flows become physical and fiscal: each flow moves real inventory from the exporting city's producers to the importing city's stockists (shortfalls recorded as city events; the flow record keeps its measured value, the goods layer reports what physically crossed), and the configured tariff is charged per flow, keyed by flow id. Settlement is idempotent per flow. Per-city trade balance is read from the flow records (Unit 11 surfaces it).
14. **Household Consumption** (`market.py`) — households buy real goods from real shelves: each settled household's food/clothing spend is allocated across its city's retail + food-service stock with **fair rationing** (every household in a city receives the same coverage fraction of its want — scarcity is shared and measured, never decided by iteration order). System retailers are paid takings for what left their shelves (owner-run shops' takings remain their settlement P&L — no double-count). Education/health lines stay honestly **0** (civic/unpriced, as Sessions 02–03). Food hardship is measurable: a household >30% unmet on food loses hunger satisfaction and records a `food_shortage` life event. The money side is unchanged — allocation spends the goods side of the settlement's existing purchase payments, never charging twice.
15. **Economic Decisions** (`decisions.py`) — new candidates, all gated on the economy layer existing (hand-built worlds see none of them): **job change** to a better-paying open vacancy (+15% wage gate, 90-day cooldown), **invest** surplus in the most profitable recorded business (risk-tolerance gated), **bank deposit** of idle cash (discipline-driven), **mortgage purchase** of the rented home (design deposit from own funds + a credit-checked bank loan for the rest). Business founding was reworked to split the existing founding cost (unchanged total) into real business capital + founding costs, registered with category/status and a `registered` event. Business-side decisions (hiring, production, closure) live in Units 03/05/04 and are logged as business events; every person-side choice lands in the existing `decision_log` with its outcome.
16. **Observatory** (`econview.py` + CLI) — data layer only: `--economy` (foundation, indicators + methodology, bank + reconciliation, labour, prices, top businesses by worth, active crises, recent events), `--business <id>` (P&L history, inventory, employees, shareholders, events), `--bank`, `--indicators <scope>`, `--finances <character_id>`. All computed live from records. No dashboard is fabricated; interactive charts remain a future UI layer over these payloads.

## Limitations (honest)

- **Unemployment reads ~0 at month boundaries in small worlds:** the pre-existing daily job-seeking decision absorbs the unemployed pool before the monthly labour pass meets, so organic applications are rare at these populations; the labour market's mechanics (posting gate, matching, business-paid wages, separations) are unit-tested directly, and measured unemployment responds correctly when jobs disappear (crisis test: mass layoff → 90%+ measured → crisis opens).
- **Investment/dividends are thin organically** over 90 days (the invest candidate needs ≥150k cash + risk tolerance; few agents qualify that fast). Mechanics are unit-tested end-to-end (valuation, purchase, pro-rata dividend from recorded profit).
- Some owner-profession businesses have no commercial unit (property_id null) when no unit matches their category/zone — they operate through the settlement P&L as before.
- Government finance attributes national taxes to Rupsha's row because `SYS-CITY` is a single national treasury account; only locatable revenue is split per city. A per-city treasury is a Session 05 (government) question, not a finance-layer one.
- Trade goods are the two Session 03 flow goods (manufactured goods, agricultural produce); other goods move via the supply chain, not the intercity flow records.
- Bonds/funds do not exist (Unit 09 models only equity in real businesses); business expansion decisions (new premises/products) are not modelled — hiring, production, pricing and closure are.
- Education/health consumption stays 0 (civic services are unpriced in the design data) — recorded as 0, never invented.
