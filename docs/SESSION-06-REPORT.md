## What was built

- **Education framework** (`data/education-health-config.json`, `institutions.py`) — a configurable per-country framework as data: sectors (public/community — the sectors the building data actually contains), the Ministry of Education authority from Session 05's institutions, academic calendar (30-day months, term examinations in months 3/6/9/12), curricula as config (school subjects + the college programmes for civil_engineer / doctor / software_developer / teacher, each subject with objectives, skill, difficulty, duration, assessment, prerequisites — writing only into the canonical `character_skills` vocabulary under the GDD-11 caps, which remain contract in `education.py`).
- **Institutions** (`institutions.py`) — a registry of exactly the institutions whose buildings exist: District School, District College and College Library in Rupsha, Shibnagar Primary School in Shibnagar (4 institutions). Institution types with no building (kindergarten, university, technical institute…) exist in the config's type framework only — **no institution row is ever created for a building the world does not have**. Each institution carries capacity, departments, a monthly civic budget authority, measured performance (latest examination mean, blended with research capability for the college) and its building's measured condition.
- **Teachers** (`institutions.py`) — real citizens employed as the teacher profession (genesis / Session 04 civic labour / Session 05 civil service), assigned monthly by residence city (config rule: in Rupsha the college takes lecturer posts first, 1 per 10 enrolled college students, at least 1 while anyone is enrolled) and evaluated from the institution's MEASURED student outcomes (examination mean 60% + measured attendance 40%; before any examination exists, the teacher's own measured teaching skill is the documented fallback). This module moves no money: teachers are paid by the existing payroll.
- **Student journeys** (`academics.py`, on Session 02's enrolment records — an enrolled record IS the student) — per-subject learning records whose proficiency grows monthly from the record's measured attendance and the institution's measured teaching quality; monthly assignments (set vs completed, derived from measured attendance + proficiency); batch examinations in exam months where **every result is a pure function of stored, measured inputs** (attendance rate, proficiency, the month's teacher score, the building's measured condition) plus a seeded jitter keyed by (exam, student, subject) — all inputs, jitter included, are stored on the result row so any grade can be recomputed exactly (acceptance criterion 5, test-pinned). Certifications are issued on completion only when the measured examination history supports them — a record can complete WITHOUT a certificate, and the two facts are kept distinct. Dropout is rule-based on measured records only (household debt in two consecutive monthly snapshots + below-pass performance for school students aged 14+; two consecutive months of measured low attendance for college students) — never a dice roll against a child. The college credential continues to gate hiring through Session 04's labour machinery (graduation → certification → eligibility proven end-to-end in the seeded acceptance world).
- **Health: profiles & the condition layer** (`health.py`) — GDD-12's design contract implemented in code for the first time: a condition is a band (unwell / injured / serious) + a cause + a course + a memory, never a random punishment. The cause list is CLOSED and validated at construction: weather exposure (read from the day's persisted weather preset + GDD-07 flags), commute incident, construction-site accident (higher rate on uninspected sites), age-related events, complications of untreated conditions — plus communicable disease, which arrives WITH the system that causes it (below), exactly as GDD-12's rule requires. Treated courses use GDD-12 §4's windows; untreated conditions roll for complication at window end; a treated condition never complicates. Effects run through existing machinery only: the scheduler's rest bend (now bending study blocks too — GDD-11 §2's illness absence), the emotions system's own 'illness' profile (defined in Session 01, never fired until now), the healthcare need, episodic memories, and the GDD-12 §3.4 mortality rule (Session 06's `lifecycle.py` hook multiplies `death_probability` for Elders with an active/recent serious condition by the configured 3.0; it is exactly 1.0 for everyone else and in worlds without the layer, so pre-Session-06 behaviour is unchanged there). Health profiles are a monthly aggregate per citizen (documented formula over needs satisfactions, age band, active conditions, chronic status) — a simulation index, not a medical assessment. Chronic (noncommunicable) onset is a monthly seeded roll for citizens aged 50+.
- **Healthcare network** (`healthcare.py`) — the network grows honestly from the data: exactly two clinics (District Clinic, Shibnagar Clinic), because those are the clinic buildings in the merged building data. Hospital/specialised types exist in the framework only; serious cases are stabilised at the clinic and referred off-district (an admission record with transfer days), never treated in an invented ward. Staff are real citizens employed as doctors (a facility with no doctors still registers patients — they wait on the untreated course; the clinic does not conjure staff — and its daily capacity falls to the configured base). Patient journeys are one queue with recorded stages: registered → assessed (a named doctor, a diagnosis roll against their measured diagnosis skill, GDD-12 §4.3; a failed diagnosis lengthens the recovery window and schedules a follow-up) → treated (the condition's fictional medication consumed from stock when one applies) → closed when the condition's course ends. Life-stage care runs through the same queue: a birth life-event produces maternal + infant care records at the mother's city facility (consuming a maternal kit when stocked), elders with a chronic profile receive a monthly chronic review, enrolled children receive a health check each exam term.
- **Medication & pharmacy** (`healthcare.py`, config) — a FICTIONAL simulated inventory (invented product names — Simparol, Nexocillin, Bandex, Hydraline, Vaxsim, Stabilon, Matrisafe): quantities per facility, procured monthly from the market pool against the clinic's own budget authority — ledger transfers SYS-CITY → SYS-MARKET with transfer keys and event refs, capped so spend never exceeds the month's allocation (`healthcare_budgets` records allocated vs procured per facility per month). A stockout leaves the visit waiting and the condition untreated: availability measurably affects care. **Care is free at point of use (GDD-12 §6) — no patient is ever charged and no per-patient ledger row exists**; the config's unit costs are the clinic's internal procurement rates (the Session 03 road-repair-rate precedent), never a price to a patient.
- **Disease simulation** (`disease.py`) — a deliberately small, configurable epidemiological model in the spec's own terms: one fictional disease construct (seasonal flu, simulated) with configured transmission/recovery/infectious/immunity parameters. Transmission runs over REAL contact structure only — household co-residents plus a seeded sample of institution-mates for enrolled students; no invented contact network exists. Recovery is scheduled at infection; recovery grants time-limited recorded immunity; vaccination protects with the configured effectiveness, resolved once per citizen per disease on a seeded stream. Index cases arise from the labelled endemic daily chance and from deterministic configured scenarios (config list fired by day_index — the government-config pattern; the standard config's scenario list is EMPTY, so no outbreak is authored into the standard world). Surveillance is monthly: active cases per 100 residents per city are measured from `disease_records`; at or above the configured threshold the city's facility runs a vaccination campaign from its own vaccine stock (doses bound coverage), prioritising children and elders, recorded dose by dose. **Scenario outputs are not real medical predictions, and no real disease is named anywhere.**
- **Wellbeing** (`wellbeing.py`) — a monthly snapshot per living citizen computed ONLY from state earlier sessions already maintain: Session 01 emotional state (stress, loneliness), Session 02's relationship graph + household + community membership (social support), the needs system's sleep/rest satisfactions (work-life proxy). No diagnosis of any kind is made or implied.
- **Research ecosystem** (`research.py`) — projects at the District College (the only higher-education institution in the building data; the framework supports universities where a later district builds one), led by real lecturer citizens, funded from a configured share (15%) of the college's monthly civic budget authority, running a configured 3–6 months, completing into `research_records` (publications as simulated records). Completion raises the college's measured research capability, which feeds its institution performance — the configurable institutional-development effect the spec requires.
- **Human development indicators** (`humandev.py`) — computed monthly from persistent records only, by the documented methodology in the module (also surfaced by the observatory): literacy rate (measured proxy: subject_knowledge ≥ configured level, or a completed school record, among residents 15+), education access (enrolled school-age / all school-age), graduation rate (trailing-12-month cohort-outcome share; absent when neither completions nor dropouts exist), skill distribution (mean/median of each living adult's highest skill), healthcare access (share of residents whose city has an active facility), healthcare capacity per 100 residents, population health + mean wellbeing, research activity, and life expectancy reported TWO ways — the observed mean age at death over recorded deaths, and a model estimate (65.61 years) integrated from `lifecycle.py`'s own mortality curve. The composite HDI is the geometric mean of health/education/income indices (income from Session 04's latest national GDP per capita, log-scaled between configured bounds). **Nothing is interpolated: a component that cannot be computed is absent, not estimated** — a world with no economic indicators gets no HDI (test-pinned).
- **Observatory** (`humandevview.py`, `__main__.py`) — `--education / --health / --development` CLI + JSON over the real records, in the `--government` pattern. Data layer only; no fabricated dashboards.

## Limitations (honest)

- **No organic college enrolment in unseeded worlds.** Genesis citizens do not spontaneously choose college (pre-existing Session 02 decision behaviour), so graduations, certifications, lecturer posts and research projects occur organically only when enrolment happens; in the 200-day scenario all four read 0. Every one of those chains is proven end-to-end in the seeded acceptance world (the same fixture action Session 02's tests use), but a world left to itself currently produces school journeys only. Making college aspiration a citizen decision is future work, not claimed here.
- **Vaccination campaigns are threshold-gated and did not fire in the 200-day scenario** (prevalence below the 4.0/100 threshold at its surveillance points) — but one fired organically in the 370-day acceptance world (71 vaccinations), and immunity/vaccine effectiveness are additionally unit-tested with configured scenarios. Transmission/endemic spread is organically proven in both worlds.
- **The network is two clinics and four institutions — the buildings that exist.** Hospitals, universities, kindergartens and the other spec types are framework entries only; serious cases leave the district by referral record. Growing the network requires buildings (Session 03 construction) — not fabricated here.
- **Observed life expectancy is a small-n statistic** (one recorded death in the scenario); the model estimate (65.61) is the documented headline figure.
- **Literacy reads low in young worlds** (0.3731) because the proxy measures a skill genesis adults largely start without; it is a measured record of the world's state, not a tuned dial.
- **Performance cost is real:** the daily health pass touches every living citizen every day (onsets, transmission contacts, queues, courses). It is the main reason the full-population benchmark grows over Session 05's; the spec's own guidance (aggregated population health, background epidemic simulation) is followed for the monthly passes, but the daily pass is per-citizen by design (conditions are individual facts).
- **Environment note (not a sim defect):** this VM's `/tmp` is a 512 MB tmpfs and the test fixtures leak ~1 MB temp databases per store, so the suite must run with `TMPDIR` on disk; two of the finisher's early aggregate runs failed on "database or disk is full" for exactly this reason before the workaround. The fixtures predate this session.
