## Platform Administrator ≠ Virtual Government (owner's hard rule)

The platform administrator (the owner, operating this repository and the Muse platform) and the virtual government (institutions inside the simulation) are **separate identities that never share a permission system**. In this codebase that separation is structural: every government authority is *in-simulation data* — rows in `institutions` (authority lists) and `offices` (holders), checked inside sim code paths by `government.require_authority`. Nothing in the simulation grants, reads, or touches any real platform access; no sim office maps to any real account, role, or credential; and no platform permission was used, changed, or modelled by this session's work. A sim "prime minister" can sign a sim trade agreement and nothing else, anywhere.

## The treasury question (deferred by Session 04) — answered

Session 04 left `SYS-CITY` as a single account and named per-city treasuries "a Session 05 question". Session 05 splits them **with conservation proven**: `SYS-CITY` remains the *national* treasury (national taxes already flow there), and two municipal treasury accounts were added — `SYS-TREASURY-RUPSHA`, `SYS-TREASURY-SHIBNAGAR` — as balance-enforced character accounts (they are deliberately *not* in persistence `SYSTEM_ACCOUNTS`, so a municipality cannot spend money it does not hold). Each city's opening position is its cumulative net position from Session 03's own `municipal_budgets` records, moved through the ledger with idempotency keys and event refs; a negative position opens at 0 with the difference recorded as `opening_advance_ncr`. A monthly allocation pass then moves *located* revenue (rent by the payer household's district, commercial rent by the property's district, property tax by the taxed property) from the national account to the cities. The conservation identity is test-pinned: after setup and after every pass, `ledger_sum_check()` is empty and total balances equal the ledger exactly.

## Units

- **01 National Government Foundation** (`government.py`, `data/government-config.json`) — one real country, **Bangladesh** (grounded in the citizens' own `nationality` data), capital Rupsha City, containing both cities; 15 institutions, leadership offices, terms, governance records (`government_events`). The data model supports multiple countries — proven by constructing a second *minimal* country record in tests; no second populated nation was fabricated.
- **02 Government Organization** — executive / legislative / judicial branches, ministries, the Election Commission, the Audit Office, two municipal administrations, each with a defined authority list; `require_authority` enforces it in code (a judge approving a budget, a mayor acting for the other city, a citizen legislating — all raise `PermissionError`, all test-pinned).
- **03 Political Parties** (`politics.py`) — three genesis parties whose ideology is a configurable 5-dimension stance vector *in the config file*; leaders and founders are real citizens chosen by measured alignment; membership grows from measured support; funding moves through the ledger (founder contributions, monthly dues); support is counted from `citizen_opinion`, never asserted.
- **04 Elections** — configurable rules (12-month terms, eligibility 18+, constituencies = the two cities with 12 + 4 seats); campaigns spend real party balances (capped at 50%); each voter's choice derives from their own stance vector, their measured approval of the incumbent, campaign visibility, and a seeded jitter — **no random voting**; one ballot per voter is enforced by a UNIQUE constraint, and ballots are **immutable** (UPDATE/DELETE triggers, the same append-only discipline as the ledger); counting is a pure function of the ballot rows (recount test-pinned); largest-remainder seat allocation; government formation seats PM, president, ministers, MPs, speaker, mayors; transfer of power ends executive/legislative/municipal offices while judiciary, EC and Audit keep their terms, and every outgoing record is preserved.
- **05 Citizen Participation** — petitions, complaints and consultations as `citizen_feedback` records (consultations are opened by the decision engine and their tallies gate real policies); public meetings reuse Session 02's events; opinion and approval are computed monthly from measured experience (wage change from the ledger, employment, price inflation, city service levels, incidents, crises) and snapshotted (`opinion_snapshots`).
- **06 Policies** (`policies.py`) — policy objects (institution, budget, duration, target, expected vs measured outcome) whose effects change real behaviour: employment programme → real government vacancies hired through the civil service; welfare → top-ups to the poverty line for households in the latest `household_finances`, capped, keyed and event-referenced; housing → Session 03 construction commissioning when vacancy is below the trigger; infrastructure → real road-segment repairs at Session 03's rate toward a target condition; health → treasury-funded investment measured by doctor counts. Spend per policy is exactly the sum of its ledger rows (`policy_spent`).
- **07 Government Budget** — annual cycle on the sim calendar: at months 12, 24, … the running budget closes with measured actuals and the next opens with a forecast from trailing-12-month recorded revenue, allocations by configured shares, approval by the Speaker (or the caretaker PM before the first parliament). Expenditure runs against treasury balances; a national overdraft at budget time is formalised into an **explicit SYS-BANK loan** through Session 04's machinery (sovereign credit assessed on recorded government revenue) — public debt is reported as explicit loans + remaining implicit overdraft, never double-counted (test-pinned).
- **08 Public Administration** (`admin.py`) — civil servants are real citizens: government vacancies (Shibnagar police and doctors per 100 residents, housing engineers, municipal engineers) are matched with the labour market's own eligibility/scoring (credential gates included), a hire creates the employment (treasury-paid) and the `civil_service` record together, payroll runs in the settlement's pre-pass and is taxed on what was actually received — exactly the business-wage pattern; evaluations come from measured institutional output; promotions raise the tier within canonical wage bands; transfers are recorded.
- **09 Law & Legislation** (`justice.py`) — constitution and legal bounds are config data; proposals (office holders only) → recorded debate (party positions from stances) → MP vote → enactment from the next month. Enacted laws change real behaviour through accessors read at action time: **sales tax** (engine purchase split), **import tariff** (trade settlement), **wage floor** (vacancy posting uplift), **migration openness** (Session 03 migration cap) — each defaulting to exactly the pre-Session-05 behaviour when no law exists, and the sales-tax delta is proven end-to-end in the acceptance test (a 105,000 NCr purchase pays 5,000 tax at the configured 5%, then 7,778 after an 8% law takes effect).
- **10 Judiciary** — cases arise only from real disputes: defaulted loans and business wage shortfalls (filed by court intake), escalated Session 02 conflicts (referred by the police). The **allegation and the finding are separate columns** — a filed case has no finding until a judge (whose office is authority-checked) writes one from the evidence record itself; remedies execute through the ledger and keep the underlying book (the loan row) consistent; one appeal, by the losing party only, heard by a different judge under a stricter evidence threshold, with refunds on overturn.
- **11 Law Enforcement & Public Safety** (`safety.py`) — police institutions staffed by real police_officer citizens (Session 05 civil-service officers plus the Session 04 civic establishment resident in the city). Incidents emerge **only** from records: escalated conflicts, loan defaults, and measured road risk (storm days × degraded segment condition, configured SIM MODEL rate). **No arbitrary crime generation exists in the code.** Processing is report → investigation by a named officer → court referral or recorded closure; safety statistics are counts over these records and feed opinion.
- **12 Corruption & Accountability** (`integrity.py`) — the monthly audit *re-runs* Session 04's reconciliation (`finance.verify_conservation` + per-account cache checks) over the government accounts and records the result under the Auditor General; conflict-of-interest flags are a data join (office holders who own Session 04 businesses); whistleblower reports are investigated by re-checking the subject account's ledger sums — discipline requires a *measured* discrepancy, anything else closes as unsubstantiated on the record; the integrity index is 100 minus a configured penalty per measured discrepancy. Nothing invents corruption events.
- **13 International Relations** (`diplomacy.py`) — the external counterpart is an explicitly labelled **model**: `External Trade Partner (modelled)`, a system account seeded once from SYS-MARKET (labelled genesis funding), with a relations record — never a fabricated populated nation (the world still contains exactly one country). Agreements are records with measurable effects: a trade agreement subtracts 2 points from the effective tariff while active (read by `trade.settle_trade` every settlement); a cooperation agreement pays a monthly grant from the partner's finite balance through the ledger; only the executive may sign (authority-checked). Migration policy reaches the sim through the law book (Unit 09).
- **14 National Development** — a development plan whose targets (unemployment, poverty, road condition, doctors per 100, housing vacancy band) are measured **only** from Session 04 indicators and sim facts; plan-vs-actual is computed from the same fact surface as the baseline.
- **15 Government Decision Engine** — a rule table over measured conditions (indicator unemployment/poverty, housing pressure, road condition, doctor density): the first firing opens a citizen consultation; a supported consultation lets the PM propose + approve the matching policy; an opposed one blocks it — every step written to `decision_log` and `government_events` (test-pinned, including the consultation gate).
- **16 Observatory** (`govview.py`, `__main__.py`) — `--government / --election / --policy / --budget / --court / --diplomacy` CLI + JSON over the real records. Data layer only; no fabricated dashboards.

## Database changes (SQLite additive only; `data/schema.sql` untouched)

As with Sessions 02–04, the sim's tables live in `tools/sim/persistence.py`; `data/schema.sql` (the design schema) is unchanged and the gap is recorded here.

**A persistence bug found and fixed by this session:** `Store.next_id` seeded its in-memory counter from `COUNT(*)` at first use in a process. Once literal-ID rows (party accounts, municipal treasuries, the partner account) are inserted *after* population generation, a resumed process re-seeded from a larger count and minted different citizen IDs than an uninterrupted run — silently breaking the Session 01 restart-equivalence guarantee (caught by the pre-existing restart test). The counter now seeds from the **maximum existing numeric suffix** for the (prefix, table) pair, which also fixes the latent `PH` prefix collision between `price_history` and `parcel_history`. Restart equivalence is re-proven by the untouched Session 01 test and by the Session 05 acceptance restart test.

## Limitations (honest)

- **Organic civil-service hiring is rare in current worlds:** the gated professions' credentialed citizens are almost always already employed, so government posts (engineers especially) stay open for long stretches; recruitment and treasury payroll are proven by fixture tests, and the open vacancies are visible in the data rather than filled by fiat.
- **The genesis fiscal year has no budget:** budgets open at month 12 for fiscal year 2; year 1 runs on recorded actuals (the budget pass keeps the open budget's actuals current monthly once one exists).
- **Session 04 civic hires change payer in government worlds:** civic employments carry `employer_character_id = 'SYS-CITY'`, so in a government world their wages are paid by the national treasury pre-pass (and taxed on receipt) instead of the SYS-EMPLOYERS pool — intended (government payroll through the treasury), and behaviour in non-government worlds is byte-identical to Session 04.
- **Health policy** is treasury funding + measured doctor counts; there is still no priced care (consistent with Sessions 02/04: no medicine price exists and none was invented).
- **Safety** has no general crime rate by design: only escalated conflicts, defaults, and measured road risk produce incidents. Cities with no police coverage see incidents wait, recorded, rather than being processed by nobody.
- **Diplomacy** has exactly one modelled counterpart and two agreement kinds; the second-country data support is deliberately only a data model.
